# Chapaev / Чапаев

**Year:** 1934
**Directors:** Georgii and Sergei Vasiliev (the "Vasiliev Brothers" — not actually brothers, just shared a pseudonym)
**Period:** High Stalinism / canonical [[Socialist Realism]]
**Runtime:** 94 min

## Plot
Civil War. The peasant commander Vasilii Chapaev, naturally talented but ideologically unformed, is assigned a [[Commissar]] (Furmanov) who educates him into Bolshevik consciousness. Chapaev gradually becomes a true Soviet hero. He is killed by Whites at the Ural River. THE foundational SR film: every Stalinist novel/film rewrites this plot.
