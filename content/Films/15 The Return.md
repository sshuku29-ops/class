# The Return / Возвращение

**Year:** 2003
**Director:** Andrei Zvyagintsev (b. 1964 Novosibirsk; debut feature)
**Cinematography:** Mikhail Kritchman
**Period:** [[Putin Era]] / Post-Soviet auteur cinema
**Runtime:** 105 min
**Award:** Venice Golden Lion 2003

## Plot
Two brothers, Andrei (~14) and Ivan (~12), live with their mother in a coastal Russian town. Their Father (Konstantin Lavronenko) returns after 12 years' absence — no explanation given. Mother gives them to him for a road trip; they travel to a remote island. Tensions escalate. The Father climbs an abandoned wooden watchtower to retrieve Ivan, who has fled there in fear; the platform breaks; Father falls and dies. The boys row him back across the lake; the boat sinks with the Father in it as they turn away. Closing b&w photo sequence over folk song "Not from a Garden" (Stavropol ethnographic recording, two falcon-brothers leaving home).
