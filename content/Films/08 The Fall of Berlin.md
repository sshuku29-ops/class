# The Fall of Berlin / Падение Берлина

**Year:** 1949
**Director:** Mikhail Chiaureli
**Period:** Late Stalinism / [[Cineanemia]]

## Plot
Stalinist WWII epic. The Soviet steel-worker Aleksei Ivanov fights through the Great Patriotic War. Stalin appears as a quasi-divine architect of victory, descending from a plane in white uniform to the cheering liberated masses of Berlin. The most complete construction of the [[Cult of Stalin]] in cinema. Khrushchev's Secret Speech specifically attacks this film.

## Concepts
- [[Cult of Stalin]] — most fully constructed here. Stalin descends in white uniform.
- [[Cineanemia]] — late Stalinist film famine (1946–1953). Only 9–15 films/year. Chiaureli's Stalin trilogy dominated.
- [[Zhdanovshchina]] (1946) — anti-cosmopolitan campaign that produced cineanemia
- [[Khrushchev Secret Speech]] — directly attacks Fall of Berlin: "only Stalin acts, issuing orders from a hall in which there are many empty chairs… Stalin acts for everybody"
- [[Brothers and Sisters Speech]] — Stalin's wartime pivot from Father to brother (1941); reverses after Stalingrad (1943)
- Grand Style aesthetics — monumental, hagiographic
