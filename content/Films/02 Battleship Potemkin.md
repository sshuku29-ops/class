# The Battleship Potemkin / Броненосец Потёмкин

**Year:** 1925
**Director:** Sergei Eisenstein
**Period:** Revolutionary 1920s
**Runtime:** 74 min

## Plot
1905 sailor mutiny aboard the Potemkin. Sailors refuse rotting maggot-infested borscht; officers order ringleaders shot; Vakulenchuk shouts "Brothers! Who are you shooting?" — squad lowers rifles. Mutiny succeeds; Potemkin sails to Odessa with Vakulenchuk's body. Civilians mourn on the docks; Cossacks massacre them on the Odessa Steps. Tsarist squadron sent to destroy Potemkin lets it pass — revolutionary brotherhood across gun barrels. Commissioned for the 20th anniversary of 1905.
