# Volga-Volga / Волга-Волга

**Year:** 1938
**Director:** Grigorii Aleksandrov
**Period:** High Stalinism / Stalinist musical comedy
**Runtime:** 104 min

## Plot
Amateur Soviet performers from a provincial town travel to Moscow for the Musical Olympiad. Strelka, a postwoman, has written a song called "Volga-Volga." She faces sabotage and bureaucratic obstruction but ultimately wins. Stalin's favorite film. Score by [[Isaak Dunaevsky]]. The Stalinist musical at full hysterical bloom.
