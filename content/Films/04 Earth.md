# Earth / Земля

**Year:** 1930
**Director:** Aleksandr Dovzhenko
**Period:** Early Stalinism (Ukrainian poetic cinema)
**Runtime:** 88 min

## Plot
A Ukrainian village during collectivization. Grandfather dies under the apple tree. The first tractor arrives. The Komsomol leader Vasyl is murdered by the [[Kulak]] Khoma. Mythopoetic vision of biological cycle (death, harvest, birth) overlapping with class struggle.

## Concepts
- [[Monumental Tableau]] — Dovzhenko's static-but-charged composition. Bodies, landscape, ritual frozen in painterly arrangement. Death and harvest as biological cycle. Example: grandfather's death under the apple tree.
- [[Kulak]] — pre-revolutionary "rich peasant" landowner. Bolshevik enemy class. Khoma is a kulak.
- "Biologism" / pantheism — critics' charge against Dovzhenko: reduces class struggle to nature
- Collectivization (1929–) — the mass forced agricultural reorganization Earth depicts
