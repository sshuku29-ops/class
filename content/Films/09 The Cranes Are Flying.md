# The Cranes Are Flying / Летят журавли

**Year:** 1957
**Director:** Mikhail Kalatozov
**Cinematographer:** Sergei Urusevsky (student of Constructivist Aleksandr Rodchenko)
**Period:** [[Khrushchev Thaw]] / Soviet New Wave
**Runtime:** 94 min
**Award:** Cannes Palme d'Or 1958

## Plot
Veronika ("Squirrel") and Boris are in love when WWII begins. Boris enlists; later dies at the front saving a wounded comrade. During a Moscow air raid, Boris's cousin Mark assaults Veronika and pressures her into marriage; she submits in a state of trauma. Veronika eventually leaves Mark; at the war's end, returning veterans gather; Boris does not return. The film closes with Veronika's recognition that he is dead.
