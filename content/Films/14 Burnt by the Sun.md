# Burnt by the Sun / Утомлённые солнцем

**Year:** 1994
**Director:** **Nikita** Mikhalkov (NOT Sergei — Sergei was his father, Soviet anthem lyricist)
**Period:** Post-Soviet (pre-Putin)
**Runtime:** 135 min
**Awards:** Cannes Special Grand Jury Prize 1994; Oscar Best Foreign Language Film 1995

## Plot
June 1936, two months before the Great Terror's first show trial. Colonel Sergei Petrovich Kotov (Mikhalkov himself), Civil War hero, lives at his pre-Revolutionary intelligentsia wife Marusia's family dacha with their 6-year-old daughter Nadia (Mikhalkov's real daughter). Marusia's former lover Mitia returns — now an OGPU/NKVD agent. He spends a day reactivating his presence in the family before arresting Kotov in the evening. Closing titles: Kotov shot 12 August 1936; Marusia died in the camps 1940; Nadia rehabilitated 1956; Mitia slit his wrists in a Moscow bathtub.
