# Daydreams / Грёзы

**Year:** 1915
**Director:** Evgenii Bauer
**Period:** Pre-Revolutionary Russian cinema
**Runtime:** 39 min

## Plot
Nedelin obsesses over his dead wife by fixating on her double, the actress Tina. Tina manipulates him; eventually he strangles her with his wife's preserved braid. Aestheticized Symbolist decadence.

## Prof's questions (study sheet)
- Nedelin's conflicting perceptions of his wife and Tina → changing gender roles in pre-revolutionary Russia
- Who is the victim: Nedelin or Tina?

## Concepts
- Pre-Revolutionary Russian cinema (Symbolist decadence, gender anxiety)
- The double / the doppelgänger as a pre-revolutionary anxiety figure
- [[Male Gaze]] — applies retroactively; Tina is a textbook to-be-looked-at body whose performance Nedelin's gaze structures
