# Little Vera / Маленькая Вера

**Year:** 1987
**Director:** Vasilii Pichul
**Period:** [[Perestroika]] / [[Glasnost]] / [[Chernukha]]
**Runtime:** 110 min
**Distinction:** First Soviet film with explicit sex scene

## Plot
A working-class family in a Soviet industrial port town (Zhdanov, now Mariupol). Vera, her alcoholic father, her abusive boyfriend Sergei. The family disintegrates: father stabs Sergei in a drunken fight; the system that promised Soviet abundance has produced only chernukha — alcoholism, violence, sexual exhaustion. The Pushkin epigraph "cherish your youthful innocence" plays as bitter irony — Vera has no innocence to cherish.
