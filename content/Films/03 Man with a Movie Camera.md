# The Man with a Movie Camera / Человек с киноаппаратом

**Year:** 1929
**Director:** Dziga Vertov (born David Abelevich Kaufman; pseudonym means "spinning top")
**Period:** [[NEP]] / Constructivism
**Runtime:** 75 min

## Plot
A day in the life of a Soviet city. No actors, no intertitles, no script — the "three no's." The cameraman himself is the protagonist. Constructivist documentary that intercuts factory machinery, women waking, traffic, sports, machines, eyes, lenses.

## Concepts
- [[Kino-Eye]] — camera as eye superior to the human one; truth-machine
- [[Life as it is]] — filming reality without staging or actors; opposite of fiction film ("opium for the people")
- [[Electric Man]] — humans + machines fused into a single optimized organism. Examples: woman washing intercut with city machinery being washed by hose-jets; her blinking intercut with camera shutter focusing
- [[Factory of Facts]] — cinema as documentary mass-production of revolutionary truth
