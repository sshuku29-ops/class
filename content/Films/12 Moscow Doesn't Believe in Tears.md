# Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears / Москва слезам не верит

**Year:** 1979 (released 1980)
**Director:** Vladimir Menshov
**Period:** [[Brezhnev Stagnation]] / Developed Socialism
**Runtime:** 160 min (Parts 1+2)
**Award:** Oscar Best Foreign Language Film 1981; 80M domestic viewers

## Plot
1958: three provincial girls share a Moscow dorm room. Katerina is seduced by Rudolf, a TV cameraman, who abandons her when she gets pregnant. 20-year jump. Katerina is now a successful factory director and single mother to teenage Aleksandra. She meets Gosha (Goga), a working-class metalworker, on the train. They begin a relationship. When Gosha discovers Katerina out-earns him, he flees; her old friend retrieves him; the film closes with Gosha at the kitchen table while Katerina watches him eat with tears.
