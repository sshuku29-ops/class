# Intellectual Montage / Dialectical Montage

**Author:** Sergei Eisenstein
**Source:** "Cinematic Principle and the Ideogram" (1929)

## Definition
Shot A + Shot B = Idea C, generated by collision rather than continuity. Marxist dialectic applied to film form. The synthesis is an abstract idea the viewer constructs, not a narrative meaning carried by either shot alone.

## Films using it
- [[02 Battleship Potemkin]] — three Alupka Palace marble lions (sleeping, waking, rising) intercut with cannons firing on the Tsarist palace

## Example
The three marble lion statues at Alupka Palace are shot in succession (one sleeping, one waking, one roaring) and intercut with the Potemkin's cannons firing. The fast cuts make it look like a single lion waking and roaring — the abstract idea synthesized: "the masses awaken." No realistic causation; pure ideological synthesis.
