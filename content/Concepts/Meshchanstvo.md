# Meshchanstvo / Мещанство

**Source:** Russian cultural concept; Bolshevik critical category

## Definition
Russian for "petty-bourgeois philistinism" — the cramped, materialistic, sexually conventional everyday life that Bolsheviks despised. Bed and Sofa's Russian title (Tret'ia Meshchanskaia) names BOTH the street AND the moral disease.

## Films using it
- [[05 Bed and Sofa]] — meshchanstvo is the film's diagnostic target

## Example
The Russian title of Bed and Sofa is *Tret'ia Meshchanskaia* — literally "Third Petty-Bourgeois Street," but punning on "Third Stage of Meshchanstvo." The film's setting (cramped Moscow apartment with three roommates and complicated sexual tension) is meshchanstvo concentrated. The critique: NEP material life has produced petty-bourgeois domestic structure even within revolutionary politics.
