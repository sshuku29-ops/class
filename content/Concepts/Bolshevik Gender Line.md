# Bolshevik Gender Line 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Period:** 1917–1928 (revolutionary peak); reversed under Stalin (1936)

## Definition
1918 Family Code: divorce by postcard, civil marriage, equal rights. Aleksandra Kollontai's "winged Eros" / "glass of water" theory: sex should be as free as drinking water. Legal abortion 1920. All reversed under Stalin (1936). Re-legalized abortion 1955 under Khrushchev.

## Example
The Bolshevik Gender Line was the most radical revolutionary gender program in any 20th-century state. Marriage by registration, divorce by postcard, abortion on demand. The 1936 [[Anti-abortion Law 1936]] reverses it. The 1955 re-legalization partially restores it. Late-Soviet [[Equal but Different]] formalizes the retreat.
