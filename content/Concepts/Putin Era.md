# Putin Era 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Period:** 1999– (Putin appointed PM August 1999; Acting President December 31, 1999)
**Source:** Beumers, *History of Russian Cinema*; Baker & Glasser, *Kremlin Rising*

## Definition
[[Vertical of Power]], managed democracy, statism. Putin (b. 1952), KGB Lt. Col., Yeltsin appoints him PM August 1999, becomes Acting President December 31, 1999. Re-elected 2004 (71%). 2003–04 was the breakthrough year for Russian cinema; The Return won Venice Golden Lion 2003.

## Films using it
- [[15 The Return]] (2003) — the era's signature post-Soviet auteur film

## Example
Beumers identifies 2003–04 as Russian cinema's breakthrough year — partly because the Putin-era economic stabilization restored film-industry funding, partly because international festivals were ready to recognize post-Soviet auteurs. The Return's Golden Lion is the first major post-Soviet Russian festival win in this register.
