# Socialist Realism (SR)

**Source:** 1934 First All-Union Congress of Soviet Writers; codified in Soviet cultural policy

## Definition
The only legitimate Soviet artistic mode after 1934. "Realistic in form, socialist in content." Combines depiction of "what is" with what "ought to be"; positive heroes; party-mindedness (partiinost'); accessibility to the masses. Replaces 1920s avant-garde experimentation.

## Films using it
- [[06 Chapaev]] — the canonical SR film
- [[07 Volga-Volga]] — Stalinist musical SR
- [[08 The Fall of Berlin]] — late-Stalinist Grand Style SR

## Example
SR demands a positive hero, a guiding elder, a journey to consciousness, and an ideologically affirmative ending. Chapaev (1934) became the template literally weeks after the Writers' Congress codified SR. Every subsequent Stalinist film either follows the template or is suppressed.
