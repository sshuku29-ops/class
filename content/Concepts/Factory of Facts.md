# Factory of Facts

**Author:** Dziga Vertov
**Source:** "The Factory of Facts" (1926)

## Definition
Cinema as documentary mass-production of revolutionary truth, NOT entertainment fiction. Vertov's institutional vision: studios should produce facts the way factories produce goods. Anti-fiction, anti-bourgeois, anti-individualist.

## Films using it
- [[03 Man with a Movie Camera]] — the model implementation

## Example
MWAMC's structure is a factory output: shots of factories at work, intercut with the cameraman at work, intercut with the editor at work. Cinema produces revolutionary truth as a coordinated industrial process.
