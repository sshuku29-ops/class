# Kulak / Кулак

**Source:** Pre-revolutionary Russian social category; Bolshevik enemy class

## Definition
Pre-revolutionary "rich peasant" landowner. Bolshevik enemy class. Stalinist collectivization (1929–) deported and killed millions labeled kulaks ("dekulakization"). Earth's antagonist Khoma is a kulak who murders Vasyl.

## Films using it
- [[04 Earth]] — Khoma is THE filmic kulak

## Example
Earth dramatizes the class conflict of collectivization. The young Bolshevik Vasyl drives the village's first tractor; the kulak Khoma murders him out of class hatred. Dovzhenko's film celebrates collectivization as the resolution; the actual historical reality (Holodomor, mass deportation, millions dead) is offscreen.
