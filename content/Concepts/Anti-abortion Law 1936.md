# Anti-abortion Law (1936)

**Source:** Soviet Council of People's Commissars decree, 27 June 1936

## Definition
Reversed NEP-era abortion legalization (1920). Pronatalism replaced revolutionary feminism. Stalinist cult of motherhood. The state needed bodies for industrialization and the coming war. Re-legalized 1955 under Khrushchev.

## Films using it
- [[07 Volga-Volga]] — same year as the Constitution; the contradiction between "equal" women and forced motherhood
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — film set June 1936, the law passes during the diegetic time
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] — implicitly: 1955 abortion re-legalization is part of the Thaw context

## Example
The 1936 anti-abortion law passed in the same year as the Stalin Constitution that proclaimed gender equality. The contradiction is constitutive of Stalinist gender ideology: women are equal citizens AND obligatory mothers. NEP's revolutionary [[Bolshevik Gender Line]] is reversed.
