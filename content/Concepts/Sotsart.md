# Sotsart / Соц-арт

**Source:** Late-Soviet artistic movement (Komar & Melamid; Vitaly Komar; Aleksandr Melamid)

## Definition
"Socialist Art" (Sots-Art). Late-Soviet ironic appropriation of Socialist Realist iconography — like Pop Art parodying consumerism. Komar & Melamid. Little Vera's Soviet kitsch-as-trash is sotsart-adjacent.

## Films using it
- [[13 Little Vera]] — the soviet imagery (slogans, wallpaper, clothes) decoupled from ideological meaning

## Example
Where Stalinist visual culture deployed banners, slogans, and Lenin imagery as ideological commitment, sotsart deploys the same imagery ironically — as found objects, as kitsch, as evidence of the system's exhaustion. Little Vera's mise-en-scène treats Soviet décor this way: present, drained, parodic.
