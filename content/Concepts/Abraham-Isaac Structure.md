# Abraham-Isaac Structure 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Source:** Genesis 22 (the Aqedah / Binding of Isaac); flagged in your prof's official Return study guide

## Definition
Genesis 22: God commands Abraham to sacrifice his son Isaac. Zvyagintsev INVERTS this in The Return. The Father does not offer the son; the son's flight up the watchtower triggers the Father's death. Read against Putin-era patriarchy and the absent-father generation of post-Soviet boys.

## Films using it
- [[15 The Return]] — the inverted Aqedah is the film's biblical-mythic spine

## Example
In Genesis, Abraham nearly sacrifices Isaac at God's command. In The Return, Ivan flees up the watchtower (a sacrificial high place); the Father climbs after him to retrieve him; the Father dies. The son's resistance to patriarchal authority kills the patriarch. The Aqedah inverted is the Putin-era inversion of [[Vertical of Power]] — the son refuses the Father's offering, and the Father falls.
