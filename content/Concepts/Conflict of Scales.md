# Conflict of Scales 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Author:** Sergei Eisenstein
**Source:** Eisenstein's montage writings; Soviet montage theory

## Definition
Extreme close-up cut against extreme long shot to generate emotional/ideological shock. The viewer's body absorbs the impact before the mind processes it. A specific category of [[Montage of Attractions]].

## Films using it
- [[02 Battleship Potemkin]] — Odessa Steps massacre

## Example
The cracked pince-nez of the bourgeois woman (extreme close-up) is cut against the Cossack troops massed as a geometric formation on the Steps (extreme long shot). The violence registers as scalar shock — vulnerable individual body vs. mechanized state.
