# Glasnost / Гласность

**Period:** Late 1980s (1986–1991)
**Source:** Gorbachev's reform program

## Definition
"Openness." Relaxation of censorship; public exposure of system's failures. Enables [[Chernukha]] cinema and the rediscovery of banned films (Commissar 1967 → released 1987; Brief Encounters 1968 → released 1986).

## Films using it
- [[13 Little Vera]] (1987) — produced under glasnost
- Implicitly enables late-1980s release of banned films

## Example
Brief Encounters (1968) sat shelved for 18 years. Glasnost in 1986 finally allowed its release. The same opening permits Little Vera's chernukha to reach Soviet audiences in 1987. Glasnost is the institutional precondition for the era's representational explosion.
