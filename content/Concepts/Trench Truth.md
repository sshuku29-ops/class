# Trench Truth / Deheroicizing 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Period:** Thaw vocabulary, post-1956

## Definition
"Trench truth" (окопная правда) and "deheroicizing" — new phrases that "entered common discourse" after [[Khrushchev Secret Speech]]. Cinema could now show the war from the soldier's mud, not the leader's map. Cranes Are Flying ascribes the defeat of Nazi Germany "not to Kremlin leadership but to the Soviet people" (prof's GPW lecture pptx).

## Films using it
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] — the inaugural trench-truth film
- [[10 Ivan's Childhood]] — trench truth at the philosophical extreme

## Example
Where [[08 The Fall of Berlin]] (1949) shows Stalin descending in white uniform to the cheering masses, Cranes (1957) shows Boris dying alone in a Belarusian forest, and Ivan (1962) shows the orphan-scout hanged offscreen. The war's truth is now in the trench, not at the Kremlin.
