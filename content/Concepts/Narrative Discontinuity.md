# Narrative Discontinuity

**Author:** Kira Muratova (style); concept generic to Soviet New Wave criticism

## Definition
Muratova's signature: jagged flashbacks, non-linear time, monologues that don't connect. The Soviet master plot can no longer hold the story together. Formal counterpart to [[Characterness]].

## Films using it
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] — exemplary instance

## Example
In Brief Encounters, Maxim exists only on a tape recorder Nadia eventually erases. The film cycles through fragmented memories of all three characters; the timeline cannot be assembled into a stable order. Narrative discontinuity is the formal evidence that the [[Big Soviet Family]]'s unifying plot no longer applies.
