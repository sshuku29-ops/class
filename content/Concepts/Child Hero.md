# Child Hero (Thaw)

**Source:** Thaw cinema convention; Tarkovsky as exemplar

## Definition
Recurring Thaw figure: the child as sign of innocence and renewal — the post-Stalin generation reborn. Tarkovsky DEFORMS this convention. Ivan is "hatred-driven" (Josephine Woll, cited via Prokhorov). His soul is older than his body. Albrecht Dürer's *Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse* appears in Ivan's book.

## Films using it
- [[10 Ivan's Childhood]] — the deformed child hero

## Example
A standard Thaw child-hero would be the optimistic Pioneer, the young son rebuilding the country. Tarkovsky's Ivan is the opposite: an orphan-scout who survives only in his negative-stock dream-frames, who carries Dürer's Apocalypse imagery in his book, whose entire existence is shaped by hatred for the Germans who killed his family. The Thaw's optimism breaks against Ivan's specific trauma.
