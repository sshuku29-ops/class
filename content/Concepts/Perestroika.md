# Perestroika / Перестройка

**Period:** 1986–1991
**Source:** Mikhail Gorbachev's reform program

## Definition
Gorbachev's "restructuring" — economic liberalization, glasnost, public criticism of Soviet past. Reveals Stagnation's hollowness. Ends in Soviet collapse 1991. Little Vera shows perestroika reality: poverty, alcoholism, broken families.

## Films using it
- [[13 Little Vera]] (1987) — the era's representative film
- Releases banned films from the Thaw and Stagnation eras (Brief Encounters released 1986)

## Example
Perestroika doesn't just permit chernukha — it produces it. Glasnost lifts censorship; cinema rushes to depict what the system suppressed. Little Vera's shock value (explicit sex, alcoholism, family violence) is perestroika cinema's defining register: the truth the system denied.
