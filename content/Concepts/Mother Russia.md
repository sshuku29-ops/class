# Mother Russia / Родина-мать

**Source:** Russian cultural-iconographic tradition; mutates across periods

## Definition
Feminine personification of the Russian land/nation. Endures suffering, gives birth to heroes, mourns the dead. Mutates across periods: revolutionary 1920s laboring woman → Stalinist Stakhanovite mother → Cranes' Veronika as Mother Russia at the station → Ivan's mother as ruin → Moscow's Katerina as double-burdened mother → The Return's absent woman.

## Example
The Mother Russia ideology requires a woman to embody national suffering and continuity. Each period's cinema renegotiates this requirement: Stalinism mass-produces it, Thaw individualizes it, Stagnation domesticates it, Perestroika debases it (Little Vera), post-Soviet auteur cinema (Zvyagintsev) erases it.
