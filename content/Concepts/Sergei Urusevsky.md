# Sergei Urusevsky 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Role:** Soviet cinematographer (1908–1974)

## Definition
DOP for [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957) and *The Letter Never Sent* (1959). Student of Constructivist artist Aleksandr Rodchenko. Diagonal compositions, fast-paced montage, subjective camera. The Cranes' "view from above" is nature's viewpoint — eternity's eye on the human moment.

## Films using it
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] — Urusevsky's signature work; inaugurates [[Soviet New Wave]] aesthetic

## Example
The Mark assault scene (your Sprint 3): Urusevsky drowns almost the whole frame in chiaroscuro black; Veronika's face is the only lit surface; Mark is not shown as a man but as a dark mass pressing in from the left. Low-angle Medium Close-Up. The cinematography does the political work — what the dialogue cannot say, the lighting and framing do.

## Related concepts
- [[Soviet New Wave]] — Urusevsky is its visual signature
- [[Anti-monumentalism]]
- Margolit's "Landscape, with Hero" — the secondary literature on Urusevsky's style
