# Squirrel / Бельчонок

**Source:** [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957)

## Definition
Veronika's nickname in *The Cranes Are Flying*. Boris's pet name for her — tender, intimate, pre-war. After Boris dies and Mark coerces her into marriage, Mark uses the same nickname cruelly — the tender word becomes its opposite.

## Films using it
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] — exclusive to this film

## Example
The transition of "Squirrel" from Boris's tender register to Mark's cruel one is the film's symbolic compression of what war and coercion do to female subjectivity. Same word, reversed valence — like the [[Stalinist Family + War Trope]] inverted.
