# Commissar

**Source:** Soviet military-political officer institution

## Definition
Party political officer attached to a Red Army unit during the Civil War. Educates the spontaneous peasant-soldier into ideological consciousness. The commissar IS the elder brother / father figure in Clark's [[Big Soviet Family]] structure — the bridge between Stalin-as-Father and the masses-as-children.

## Films using it
- [[06 Chapaev]] — Furmanov is THE commissar. Educates Chapaev into consciousness.

## Example
In Chapaev, Furmanov arrives at Chapaev's unit to find a brilliant but ideologically formless peasant commander. Through patient explanation (the famous "where should the commander be" potato lesson), Furmanov turns Chapaev from a Civil War talent into a Bolshevik-conscious revolutionary.
