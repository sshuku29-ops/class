# Kino-Eye / Kinoglaz

**Author:** Dziga Vertov
**Source:** "We: A Version of a Manifesto" (1922); "Cine-Eyes: A Revolution" (1923)

## Definition
The camera as eye superior to the human one. Sees what the human eye cannot — slow motion, reverse motion, microscopic, aerial, dialectically edited. Cinema's truth-machine. Vertov's foundational concept: cinema's job is to see reality MORE truly than we can.

## Films using it
- [[03 Man with a Movie Camera]] — pure Kino-Eye: no fiction, no actors, no script

## Example
In MWAMC, the cameraman lies on the tracks to capture a train passing overhead; films a woman waking through a window; intercuts machinery, eyes, and city movement at speeds the human eye cannot process. The camera assembles a Soviet city the human observer could never directly perceive.
