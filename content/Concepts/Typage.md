# Typage

**Author:** Sergei Eisenstein
**Source:** "Cinematic Principle and the Ideogram" (1929); also "Through Theatre to Cinema"

## Definition
Eisenstein's casting principle: select non-actors based on physical type, not acting skill. The face IS the social class. Worker plays worker, sailor plays sailor. No psychology — pure social typology.

## Films using it
- [[02 Battleship Potemkin]] — Vakulenchuk's worn proletarian face vs. the officers' soft, sneering, decadent features

## Example
Vakulenchuk, the doomed sailor-leader in Potemkin, is played by a worker with a weathered face — the face IS the proletarian condition. Tsarist officers are cast for soft features that read as class decadence on screen.
