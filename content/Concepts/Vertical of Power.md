# Vertical of Power / Вертикаль власти 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Source:** Putin-era political doctrine; cited in Beumers + Baker & Glasser; named in your assigned study guide

## Definition
Putin-era doctrine. Vertical chain of hierarchical authority, strong uncompromising government from the top, unconditional discipline. The Return enacts this in family form — the absent Father returns and demands obedience. Continuation of the Stalinist [[Big Soviet Family]] structure under post-Soviet branding.

## Films using it
- [[15 The Return]] — the doctrine in family form. The watchtower scene literalizes the vertical structure; its collapse externalizes the doctrine's failure.

## Example
The Return's watchtower fall is the film's most explicit Vertical of Power image. The Father climbs the literal vertical structure (the abandoned wooden tower); the platform breaks under him; the patriarch falls. Zvyagintsev's auteurist refusal of explanation lets the image work both as Putin-era allegory and as universal patriarchal myth.
