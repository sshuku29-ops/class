# Shestidesyatniki / Шестидесятники 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Author:** Lynne Attwood
**Source:** "Brezhnev: Era of Stagnation" chapter

## Definition
"Those of the sixties." Generation produced by the Thaw — exposed to the Secret Speech, Khrushchev's housing programs, Yevtushenko, samizdat, more open debates. Resistant to Brezhnev-era neo-Stalinism. Attwood: "in public they supported the system; in private they either attacked or ignored it." The new psychological breed of post-Stalin Soviet citizen.

## Films using it
- Background for [[11 Brief Encounters]] (1968)
- [[12 Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears]] (1979) — Katerina is implicitly a shestidesyatnik

## Example
The shestidesyatniki are the children of the [[20th Party Congress]] and the [[Khrushchev Secret Speech]] grown up. Under Brezhnev, they hold double lives: outwardly compliant, inwardly critical. The double life IS Stagnation's psychological structure — and Brezhnev cinema like Brief Encounters can finally film it.
