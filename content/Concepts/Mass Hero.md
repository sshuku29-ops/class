# Mass Hero

**Author:** Sergei Eisenstein (concept; refined in Soviet film theory)

## Definition
The collective body as protagonist. Cinema with no individual lead — the masses themselves drive the plot. Achieved through [[Typage]] (anonymous classed faces), [[Montage of Attractions]] (rhythmic collective movement), and [[Conflict of Scales]] (individual vs. crowd).

## Films using it
- [[02 Battleship Potemkin]] — the sailors collectively, then the Odessa crowd

## Example
Vakulenchuk dies in Act 2 of Potemkin, but the revolution continues — because the protagonist was never one man. The Odessa crowd mourning him, the sailors collectively mutinying, the Tsarist squadron's sailors refusing to fire — this is the mass hero in action.
