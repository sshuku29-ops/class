# Double Burden

**Author:** Lynne Attwood
**Source:** "Khrushchev and the Thaw" + "Brezhnev: Era of Stagnation" chapters

## Definition
Soviet/Russian women's "two virtually full-time jobs" — wage labor AND domestic labor (Attwood's exact phrasing). Khrushchev was the first leader to publicly acknowledge it. Brezhnev tried to RESOLVE it not by redistributing housework but by retreating women back from work.

## Films using it
- [[12 Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears]] — Katerina embodies it: factory director by day, single mother / cook / cleaner by night
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] — Valentina's Soviet inefficiency burden + domestic exhaustion

## Example
Katerina runs a factory by day and cooks/cleans/raises Aleksandra alone by night. The film's "solution" is Gosha's arrival — but Gosha's solution is for Katerina to put work in second place. The Double Burden is "resolved" by [[Re-traditionalization]], not by sharing labor.
