# "Second Economy" / "Second Culture" 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Author:** Lynne Attwood
**Source:** "Brezhnev: Era of Stagnation" chapter

## Definition
Brezhnev-era parallel reality. Black market replaced the failing official economy; samizdat (self-publication) replaced state press; unofficial youth groups replaced Komsomol. Attwood: "a 'second economy' and a 'second culture' took their places alongside the official Soviet versions."

## Films using it
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] — Soviet inefficiency (the water that won't flow) drives the plot
- [[12 Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears]] — Katerina's success exists despite, not because of, the official system

## Example
Under Brezhnev, official ideology and lived reality bifurcated. Officially: developed socialism, full employment, abundance. Actually: black-market consumer goods, samizdat literature, personal connections (blat) for everything. Cinema either ignored this gap (Stalinist hangover) or registered it (Muratova, Pichul).
