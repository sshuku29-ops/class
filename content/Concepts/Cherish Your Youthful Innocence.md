# "Cherish Your Youthful Innocence"

**Source:** Pushkin epigraph (Russian: "Береги честь смолоду" — closer to "guard your honor from youth")
**Film context:** [[13 Little Vera]] (1987)

## Definition
Pushkin epigraph from *The Captain's Daughter*, repeated ironically in Little Vera. Pushkin's original moral injunction is to preserve honor as one ages. In Little Vera, Vera has no innocence to cherish — the Soviet promise of youthful purity is dead.

## Films using it
- [[13 Little Vera]] — the film's foundational ironic frame

## Example
The Pushkin line is the kind of moral chestnut Soviet schools recited. Pichul's Little Vera quotes it precisely so the chernukha that follows (sex, alcoholism, family violence) reads as the Pushkin promise's collapse. The "youthful innocence" the title invokes ironically is exactly what the Soviet system has destroyed.
