# Stalinist Family + War Trope

**Author:** Alexander Prokhorov
**Source:** "Springtime for Soviet Cinema"

## Definition
Stalinist war melodrama: the woman waits faithfully for her officer/pilot husband; his survival is guaranteed by her fidelity; "the husband's return mirrors success at the front line." The trope binds female sexual fidelity to military victory.

## Films using it
- *She Defends the Motherland* (1943, refused intertext for Cranes)
- Konstantin Simonov's poem "Wait for Me" (1941) — the literary template
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957) — INVERTS this trope

## Example
Cranes flips the script: Boris is killed at the front; Veronika is "unfaithful" (forced by Mark); the Stalinist contract between female fidelity and male return is broken. This is why Cranes was so revolutionary — it refused the trope at the level of its dramatic engine, not just its surface.
