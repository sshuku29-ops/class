# Sculpting in Time

**Author:** Andrei Tarkovsky
**Source:** *Sculpting in Time* (book of essays); "The Film Image" (handout in syllabus)

## Definition
Tarkovsky's filmmaking philosophy. Cinema records lived duration, not edited shocks. Long takes, atmospheric pacing, water and fire as elemental constants. Where Eisenstein's montage assembles meaning through cuts, Tarkovsky's sculpting carves meaning out of unfolding time.

## Films using it
- [[10 Ivan's Childhood]] — the first sustained application of the philosophy
- All Tarkovsky's later films extend it

## Example
The well dream in Ivan's Childhood holds on Ivan's mother for an extended duration; Tarkovsky lets the time itself do the emotional work. Where a Stalinist film would cut to a flag, Tarkovsky holds on water dripping. Time is the medium, not the obstacle.
