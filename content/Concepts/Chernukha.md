# Chernukha / Чернуха

**Period:** Perestroika and post-Soviet (late 1980s onward)
**Source:** Russian cultural-cinematic style

## Definition
"Blackness." Style focused on grim everyday reality — alcoholism, abuse, decay, sex, violence. Little Vera is the foundational chernukha film. Mikhalkov pointedly OPPOSES chernukha; Burnt by the Sun is his anti-chernukha statement.

## Films using it
- [[13 Little Vera]] — foundational
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — defined AGAINST chernukha (Mikhalkov's affirming counter-position)

## Example
Little Vera's port-town setting — drunkenness, family violence, sexual exhaustion, broken communist promises — is the chernukha aesthetic concentrated. The style insists that "this is what Soviet life actually was," refusing the [[Magic Tablecloth]] of Stalinist visual tradition.
