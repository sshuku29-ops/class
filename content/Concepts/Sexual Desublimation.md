# Sexual Desublimation

**Author:** Igor Kon
**Source:** "Gender and Sexuality in Russia"

## Definition
Late Soviet/Perestroika public discussion of sex and desire after decades of Stalinist puritanism. The state's prior policy of sublimating sexuality into devotion to the Party gives way to explicit cultural representation. Little Vera embodies it: explicit sex on Soviet screen for the first time.

## Films using it
- [[13 Little Vera]] — the foundational instance
- Prefigured in [[11 Brief Encounters]]'s portrayal of female sexual subjectivity
- Background for the male-gaze scenes in [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] and [[15 The Return]]'s gender absences

## Example
Little Vera (1987) features the first explicit sex scene in Soviet cinema. The shock isn't the nudity — it's that the state has lost its monopoly on what desire can mean. Sexual desublimation under [[Glasnost]] is part of the broader exposure of "the system's flaws" the prof's exam frames.
