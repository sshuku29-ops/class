# Khrushchev Secret Speech 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Author:** Nikita Khrushchev
**Source:** "On the Cult of Personality and Its Consequences," delivered to closed session of the [[20th Party Congress]], 25 February 1956

## Definition
Khrushchev's denunciation of Stalin's crimes — show trials, mass executions, Cult of Personality. The speech split Soviet society overnight into "Stalin's heirs" and "the children of the Twentieth Congress" (Evtushenko's phrase). Triggered de-Stalinization, the [[Khrushchev Thaw]], and the [[Soviet New Wave]].

## Films using it
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957) — the first major Soviet film made under the Speech's permission
- [[10 Ivan's Childhood]] (1962)
- All Thaw cinema is post-Secret-Speech

## Example
The Secret Speech specifically attacks [[08 The Fall of Berlin]]: "only Stalin acts, issuing orders from a hall in which there are many empty chairs… Stalin acts for everybody." This is Khrushchev exposing the [[Cult of Stalin]]'s representational logic — the Stalin who does everything is the Stalin who erases everyone else.
