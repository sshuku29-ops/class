# "Dear Comrades!" Greeting / Дорогие товарищи! 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Source:** [[11 Brief Encounters]] (1968) — opening sequence

## Definition
Valentina rehearses this Party-meeting greeting alone in her empty apartment. The official voice rehearsed in domestic solitude — the gap between public ritual and private emptiness becomes the film's signature.

## Films using it
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] — exclusive to this film

## Example
The "Dorogie tovarishchi!" is supposed to be a public address to comrades. Valentina rehearses it alone, with no audience, in a cluttered apartment, hunched over her desk. The Soviet ritual of address has decayed to the point where it must be performed alone. Bleak score under the rehearsal underscores Valentina's isolation and the absurd unachievable bureaucratic tasks ("raising Soviet agriculture") offloaded onto her by incompetent and irresponsible men (the prof's exact phrasing in your Sprint feedback).
