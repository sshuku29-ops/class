# Russian Gender Duality

**Author:** Igor Kon
**Source:** "Gender and Sexuality in Russia"

## Definition
The simultaneously elevated and exploited Russian woman: idealized [[Mother Russia]] AND working woman without rights at home; revered in poetry, dominated in the kitchen. The contradiction is the system, not its accident.

## Films using it
- [[12 Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears]] — Katerina elevated professionally, exploited domestically
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — Marusia elevated as intelligentsia daughter, exploited as ground for two patriarchies' competition
- [[15 The Return]] — post-Soviet endpoint: the woman is removed from the frame entirely

## Example
Russian gender duality is the cultural structure that makes [[Equal but Different]] coherent: women can be "equal" to men in public (work, education, voting) AND "different" (i.e., unequal) at home. Kon's diagnosis: this isn't a failure of equality, it IS Russian gender ideology.
