# Stakhanovite Movement / Stakhanov and Stakhanovites

**Source:** State propaganda from 1935 onward

## Definition
Cult of the over-fulfilling worker. Named after coal miner Aleksei Stakhanov (1935), who supposedly mined 14× his quota in a single shift. State propaganda used Stakhanovites to legitimize industrial speedup — every Soviet citizen should be capable of similar excess.

## Films using it
- [[07 Volga-Volga]] — Strelka's amateur song-writing as Stakhanovite-style overachievement
- All late-1930s SR cinema implicitly invokes the model

## Example
Strelka in Volga-Volga is a postwoman who writes hit songs in her spare time and travels to Moscow to win a national music competition — Stakhanovite excess applied to amateur arts. The film celebrates the principle: every Soviet citizen should be exemplary, beyond their assigned role.
