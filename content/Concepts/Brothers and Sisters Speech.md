# "Brothers and Sisters" Speech

**Author:** Joseph Stalin
**Source:** Radio address, 3 July 1941 (12 days after Operation Barbarossa)

## Definition
Stalin's first wartime address began with the salutation "Brothers and sisters!" — pivoting Stalin from "Father" of the nation to "brother." A temporary horizontal flattening of the Big Family hierarchy needed to mobilize war effort. After the Stalingrad victory (Feb 1943), Stalin reasserts vertical Father-authority.

## Films using it
- [[08 The Fall of Berlin]] — implicitly: Stalin's reasserted Father-authority post-Stalingrad
- General Great Patriotic War context for [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] and [[10 Ivan's Childhood]]

## Example
The "Brothers and sisters" salutation is the linguistic register of [[Spontaneous De-Stalinization]] — the wartime breakdown of Stalinist hierarchy that lets the war get fought. Cranes Are Flying (1957) inherits this register: the war as the people's experience, not the leader's order.
