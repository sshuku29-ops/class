# NEP / New Economic Policy

**Period:** 1921–1928
**Source:** Lenin's economic reform after the Civil War

## Definition
Lenin's limited-capitalism reset after the Civil War's destruction. Cultural pluralism; private trade allowed; revolutionary gender experimentation continues. Bed and Sofa lives in NEP. Ends with Stalin's First Five-Year Plan (1928) and the consolidation of Stalinist control.

## Films using it
- [[03 Man with a Movie Camera]] (1929) — the late-NEP moment Vertov films
- [[05 Bed and Sofa]] (1926/27) — the policy frame that allows the film's sexual experimentation

## Example
NEP is the breathing space between revolutionary 1917–1921 and Stalinist 1928–1953. Cultural avant-garde flourishes; foreign films (the [[Inostranshchina]] love of foreign things) dominate; Bolshevik gender radicalism continues. Bed and Sofa's ménage à trois is unimaginable outside NEP.
