## Definition
Post-Soviet cinema's project of dismantling the [[Cult of Stalin]]. The tradition: *Repentance* (1986, Abuladze) → *I Served as Stalin's Bodyguard* (1989) → *Inner Circle* (1990) → [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] (1994). Repentance is foundational. Burnt joins the tradition.

## Films using it
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — the tradition's mid-1990s mainstream-prestige version

## Example
Each film in the tradition does specific anti-Stalinist work. Repentance: the founding gesture, "burying him means forgiving him." Stalin's Bodyguard: the personal-witness archive. Inner Circle: the daily-life-with-Stalin angle. Burnt by the Sun: the "father figure that never existed" — the perfect Stalinist family seen on its last day, before the system that produced it eats its own.
