# 1955 Abortion Re-legalization

**Source:** Soviet decree, 23 November 1955

## Definition
Khrushchev reversed the [[Anti-abortion Law 1936]] of Stalinism. Re-legalized abortion. One of the first official de-Stalinization moves in social policy, predating the [[Khrushchev Secret Speech]] (Feb 1956) by three months.

## Films using it
- Background for [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957) — the policy framework that allows new representation of female sexuality and reproduction
- General Thaw context

## Example
The 1955 re-legalization signals that women's reproductive autonomy was re-entering Soviet policy after 19 years of forced motherhood. It's the gender-policy precursor to the [[Khrushchev Thaw]]'s broader cultural opening — Cranes (1957) and the Soviet New Wave become possible inside this newly opened space.
