# Created Geography / Magic Geography

**Author:** Lev Kuleshov

## Definition
Editing together images from different locations creates a single fictive space the viewer accepts as real. Continuity of time, space, and action constructed by montage rather than by physical reality.

## Films using it
- Foundational concept used implicitly across Soviet cinema
- Most explicit in [[02 Battleship Potemkin]] (the Odessa Steps geography is partly created by montage rather than directly photographed)

## Example
A character looks left, then a building is shown — the viewer accepts that the character is looking at the building, even if the two shots were filmed in different cities. Soviet montage theory extends this principle into ideological terrain ([[Intellectual Montage]]).
