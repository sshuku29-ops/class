# 20th Communist Party Congress

**Date:** 14–25 February 1956, Moscow

## Definition
The Congress at which Khrushchev delivered the [[Khrushchev Secret Speech]] denouncing Stalin's crimes. The speech was technically secret but circulated widely within months. Splits Soviet society overnight into "Stalin's heirs" and "the children of the Twentieth Congress" (Evtushenko).

## Films using it
- Background for all [[Khrushchev Thaw]] cinema
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957) is the first major Soviet film released after the Congress

## Example
The 20th Party Congress is the institutional event the Thaw builds from. Cranes Are Flying's existence as a film about wartime female trauma (rather than Stalinist victory) is unimaginable before the Congress; the [[Soviet New Wave]] generation that follows calls itself the "children of the Twentieth Congress."
