# "Moral Occult"

**Author:** Peter Brooks
**Source:** *The Melodramatic Imagination* (cited via McReynolds)

## Definition
Brooks via McReynolds: "the domain of operative spiritual values, which is both indicated within and masked by the surface of reality." Melodrama lets audiences sort out the ethics of a secular world. Functional in late-Soviet life as official discourse loses moral authority.

## Films using it
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] — the moral occult speaks through Veronika's silenced body what the public discourse refuses

## Example
After the [[Khrushchev Secret Speech]] (1956), official Soviet moral discourse loses authority. Cranes (1957) restores moral knowing through the melodramatic register: Veronika's body, lit and silenced, registers what the dialogue cannot say — the war was not won, the Big Family was not restored, the woman pays.
