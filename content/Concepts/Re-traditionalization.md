# Re-traditionalization 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Period:** Late Soviet (Brezhnev / post-Soviet)
**Author:** Lynne Attwood

## Definition
Late Soviet return to "traditional" gender roles after Stalinist cult-of-motherhood and Thaw femininity. The man "leads," the woman "supports." Moscow rewards Katerina's success with submission to Gosha. Continues into post-Soviet Russia under [[Vertical of Power]].

## Films using it
- [[12 Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears]] — the era's exemplary film
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — Mikhalkov's nostalgic patriarchal frame extends this logic backward
- [[15 The Return]] — post-Soviet re-traditionalization to the point of erasing the maternal

## Example
Katerina in Moscow has spent 20 years building professional and personal autonomy. The film's resolution requires her to step back into a gender role her actual biography has outgrown. Re-traditionalization is the cultural process that makes this step backward look like reward.
