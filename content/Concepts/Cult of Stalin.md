# Cult of Stalin

**Source:** Soviet state propaganda from ~1929 onward

## Definition
Post-1929 mass-mediated worship of Stalin as father, sun, demiurge. Consolidated through portraits, statues, films, anthems, schoolbooks. Most fully constructed in cinema in [[08 The Fall of Berlin]]. Dismantled at the [[20th Party Congress]] (Feb 1956).

## Films using it
- [[07 Volga-Volga]] — Cult under construction (1938)
- [[08 The Fall of Berlin]] — Cult at its zenith (1949). Stalin descends from a plane in white uniform to the cheering liberated.
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — the Cult deconstructed retrospectively (Stalin balloon as ironic image)

## Example
[[Khrushchev Secret Speech]] specifically attacks Fall of Berlin: "only Stalin acts, issuing orders from a hall in which there are many empty chairs… Stalin acts for everybody." This is the Cult's representational logic: every Soviet act becomes a Stalin act.
