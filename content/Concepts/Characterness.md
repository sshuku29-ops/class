# Characterness / Характерность 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Author:** Boris Groys (term); applied to Muratova by Alexander Prokhorov
**Source:** Prokhorov, "Springtime for Soviet Cinema"

## Definition
Characters cycle through fragmented identities without ever cohering into a unified subject. The end of the unified Soviet hero. Where [[Spontaneity-Consciousness Dialectic]] tracked the spontaneous hero's journey to consciousness, characterness shows characters whose multiple roles never resolve.

## Films using it
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] — Valentina cycles through identities (lover, surrogate mother, water official) without ever cohering

## Example
Valentina in Brief Encounters is simultaneously a lover (of the absent Maxim), a surrogate mother (to Nadia), a senior city water official, a Party-meeting orator rehearsing "Dorogie tovarishchi!" alone. None of these identities resolves into a unified Soviet self. The Stalinist master plot's spontaneity-consciousness journey has dissolved into characterness.
