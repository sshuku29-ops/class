# Soviet New Wave

**Author:** Alexander Prokhorov (term)
**Source:** "Springtime for Soviet Cinema"

## Definition
Prokhorov's term for [[Khrushchev Thaw]] cinema (1956–1968). Anti-monumentalism, individuality, fragmented narrative, return of nature, DOPs (especially [[Sergei Urusevsky]]) as creative force, female filmmakers (Shepit'ko, Muratova). Parallel to the French New Wave but with distinct Soviet political stakes.

## Films using it
- [[09 The Cranes Are Flying]] (1957) — inaugural film
- [[10 Ivan's Childhood]] (1962) — the Wave's philosophical apex
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] (1968) — late Wave, banned

## Example
The Soviet New Wave's defining moves: subjective camera (Urusevsky in Cranes), associative editing (Tarkovsky in Ivan), fragmented narrative (Muratova in Brief Encounters). Each move is a refusal of Stalinist monumentalism.
