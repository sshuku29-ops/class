# Isaak Dunaevsky

**Role:** Soviet film composer (1900–1955)

## Definition
The composer of the Stalinist musical's signature scores. Together with director Grigorii Aleksandrov, formed the "Soviet Hollywood" team. His melodies are the era's earworms — pure Stalinist musical ear-candy that masks ideological function.

## Films using it
- [[07 Volga-Volga]] (1938) — the title song
- *Jolly Fellows* (1934)
- *The Circus* (1936)

## Example
Volga-Volga's title theme, written by Dunaevsky, became one of the most-recognized melodies in Soviet culture — Stalin reportedly sent a copy to Roosevelt. The score's relentless cheerfulness IS the [[Magic Tablecloth]]: musical abundance covering political reality.
