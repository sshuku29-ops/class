# Brezhnev Stagnation / Эпоха Застоя 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Period:** October 1964 (Brezhnev replaces Khrushchev) — March 1985 (Gorbachev)

## Definition
Neo-traditional, ideologically frozen regime. Officially called "developed socialism" at the time; "stagnation" is retrospective. Failed Cult of Brezhnev. Tightening on Thaw freedoms; consumer goods promised but rarely delivered. Resisted by [[Shestidesyatniki]]. Moscow IS Stagnation cinema.

## Films using it
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] (1968) — at the moment Stagnation begins
- [[12 Moscow Doesn't Believe in Tears]] (1979) — the era's signature film

## Example
The Stagnation's representational logic: gendered re-traditionalization (Equal but Different), parallel "second economy" (black market, samizdat), failed Cult of Brezhnev (the leader as senile placeholder), and what was officially called "developed socialism" but felt to those living it like an ideologically frozen waiting room.
