# Great Terror (1936–1938)

**Period:** August 1936 (Zinoviev–Kamenev show trial) through November 1938

## Definition
Mass NKVD arrests, show trials of Old Bolsheviks (Zinoviev and Kamenev 1936; Bukharin 1938), ~700,000 executions, ~1.5 million sent to GULAG. Stalin's consolidation of personal power through the destruction of all rivals real and imagined.

## Films using it
- [[14 Burnt by the Sun]] — film set June 1936, two months before the first show trial. Kotov is shot 12 August 1936 (per closing titles), one of the Terror's first military victims.

## Example
Burnt by the Sun's entire dramatic structure depends on the Terror as offscreen presence. Kotov is a Civil War hero, decorated, with Stalin's portrait literally floating above his dacha — and his arrest day is two months before the regime that fed him will start eating its own.
