# Mantegna's Dead Christ 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Source:** Andrea Mantegna, *Lamentation of Christ* / *Dead Christ* (c. 1480, Pinacoteca di Brera, Milan)
**Cited in:** Your prof's official Return study guide includes the painting as a reference image

## Definition
Andrea Mantegna's late-15th-century religious painting. Foreshortened body lying flat, feet toward viewer; the original includes mourning women at left margin (Mary, John, Mary Magdalene). Zvyagintsev quotes the foreshortened-body composition in shots of the dead/sleeping Father — but crops out the mourning women.

## Films using it
- [[15 The Return]] — the Father-asleep / Father-dead shots quote Mantegna directly

## Example
The shot of the boys discovering the Father asleep on the bed is a direct Mantegna citation: foreshortened body, feet toward camera, head distant. Late-Renaissance chiaroscuro lighting. The boys are out of frame; the viewer occupies the disciples' position before the dead Christ. For your gender track: Zvyagintsev's citation strips out the original painting's mourning women — the post-Soviet [[Vertical of Power]] is a Christology without a Madonna.
