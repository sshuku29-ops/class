# Zhdanovshchina 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Author:** Andrei Zhdanov (Leningrad Party head and chief Stalinist ideologist)
**Period:** 1946–1948

## Definition
Anti-cosmopolitan campaign launched by Zhdanov in 1946. Re-imposed cultural rigidity after wartime relaxation. Anti-Semitic purge of cultural and intellectual elite (1948–49 in particular). Produced [[Cineanemia]].

## Films using it
- Background for [[08 The Fall of Berlin]] — the cultural environment that made Chiaureli's Stalin-hagiography both possible and demanded

## Example
Zhdanov's 1946 attacks on Akhmatova and Zoshchenko set the cultural tone — anything insufficiently "Soviet" was branded "cosmopolitan" (a code for Jewish-influenced or Western-influenced). Cinema responded with cineanemia. The Thaw partially reverses Zhdanovshchina; the Brezhnev era's "developed socialism" partially restores it.
