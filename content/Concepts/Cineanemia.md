# Cineanemia / Малокартинье 📚

> **📚 SUPPLEMENTARY** — appears in assigned readings or lectures, useful for the essay (Option 2) and for shot analysis (Parts II + III). Not directly named on the prof's exam study sheet, so unlikely to appear as a Part I-B Option 1 question.


**Period:** 1946–1953 (late Stalinism)

## Definition
Late Stalinist film famine. Soviet studios produced only 9–15 films a year (down from 80–120 in the 1930s). Caused by the [[Zhdanovshchina]] cultural purge plus Stalin's personal preference for monumental hagiography. Chiaureli's Stalin trilogy dominated: *The Vow* (1946), *The Fall of Berlin* (1949), *The Unforgettable 1919* (1951).

## Films using it
- [[08 The Fall of Berlin]] (1949) — produced during cineanemia; one of the few films of its year

## Example
In 1951, the Soviet Union released only 9 feature films. The cineanemia period's representative texts are the Stalin-cult productions; the Thaw's first task was to break the famine and re-allow Soviet cinema to be made.
