# Electric Man / Perfect Electric Man

**Author:** Dziga Vertov
**Source:** "We: A Version of a Manifesto" (1922)

## Definition
Humans + machines fused into a single optimized organism. The Constructivist body of the new socialist worker. Vertov's utopian project: cinema reveals and helps construct a new human type — half-mechanical, half-organic, fully revolutionary.
