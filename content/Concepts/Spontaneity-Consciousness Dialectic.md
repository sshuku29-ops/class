# Spontaneity-Consciousness Dialectic

**Author:** Katerina Clark
**Source:** *The Soviet Novel: History as Ritual* (1981)

## Definition
The master plot of [[Socialist Realism]]. The young hero starts spontaneous (peasant, ignorant, instinctive); is guided by an elder mentor (commissar, party member) toward consciousness (ideological clarity). The hero's transformation is the entire narrative.

## Films using it
- [[06 Chapaev]] — IS this plot. Chapaev is the spontaneous peasant; Furmanov is the [[Commissar]] who educates him
- [[07 Volga-Volga]] — Strelka's amateur-to-Stakhanovite arc
- [[11 Brief Encounters]] — the dialectic's collapse; Valentina cycles through identities without ever cohering ([[Characterness]])

## Example
In Chapaev, the title character begins as a brilliant but intuitive Civil War commander. Furmanov, his commissar, slowly educates him: how to read a map, how to think dialectically, how to subordinate his peasant instincts to Bolshevik strategy. By the time Chapaev dies, he has achieved consciousness — that's the SR redemption arc.
