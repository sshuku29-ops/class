# Associative Linking

**Author:** Andrei Tarkovsky
**Source:** Tarkovsky's writings (handout in syllabus)

## Definition
Tarkovsky's editing principle. Shots connect by inner meaning / dream-logic, not narrative cause-effect. Apple = abundance, water = memory, fire = trauma. Builds meaning through symbolic resonance rather than continuity.

## Films using it
- [[10 Ivan's Childhood]] — the well dream, the apple cart, the negative-stock beach scenes

## Example
In Ivan's Childhood: the apple cart in the rain falls; the apples are eaten by horses on the beach; the beach is shot on negative film stock; the negative imagery encodes both abundance and apocalypse. Linked not by plot ("then this happened") but by meaning ("apple = the world Ivan lost"). The well dream similarly: the well is mother is memory is water is innocence — all linked associatively.
