# Life as it is / Life Caught Unawares

**Author:** Dziga Vertov
**Source:** Vertov's manifestos (1922 onward)

## Definition
Filming reality without staging or actors. The opposite of fiction film, which Vertov called "opium for the people." The cameraman captures truth on the fly. Both phrasings ("life as it is" and "life caught unawares") appear in Vertov's writings.

## Films using it
- [[03 Man with a Movie Camera]] — the entire film is built on this principle

## Example
Every sequence of MWAMC: real workers in real factories, real women waking in real Moscow apartments, real traffic, real eye examinations. Vertov's anti-fiction position is that staged drama is bourgeois opium; documentary cinema is revolutionary truth.
