# Cinema — RU244 Russian Cinema From Lenin to Putin

The two hubs:

- [[Films]] — all 15 films in syllabus order, year + director + period + key concepts + plot
- [[Concepts]] — all theoretical concepts, definitions, films-using-them, examples

When you click any wikilink in either hub, every concept and film auto-cross-references through Obsidian's backlinks. Open Graph View to see the whole web visually.
